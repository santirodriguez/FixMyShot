# Privacy — unreleased development preview

FixMyShot processes images locally. It requests no Internet, broad photo-library, camera or location permission. There are no accounts, advertising, analytics or subscriptions. Application backup is disabled. The Android picker or an external provider may fetch cloud files independently; FixMyShot does not upload your images. Optional links open an external browser only after you choose them.

The system Photo Picker and Android share intents provide only selected images. Supported static SDR JPEG/PNG/WebP inputs are copied to private cache for review; originals are read only. Protective limits are 100 images per batch, 100 MiB per input and 1 GiB staged sources, with lower device memory limits possible. Private staged copies may contain original identifying metadata. SHA-256 fingerprints detect changed sources. Preview and thumbnail work stays local; the thumbnail cache is bounded to 24 MiB.

Export creates new PNG or lossless WebP copies. Identifying source metadata is removed from new copies; compatible unchanged PNG/WebP images can have metadata stripped without recompressing their image payload. PNG and metadata removal are new-session defaults. This does not redact names or other personal content visible in image pixels. Metadata preservation, the complete color/HDR pipeline and native HEIC/AVIF output remain incomplete development features, not accepted capabilities.

On Android 10/API 29 or later, successful copies are published to Pictures/FixMyShot through owned MediaStore entries. Older Android uses a folder explicitly selected with the system picker, where unfinished documents can be visible during writing. Saved copies remain under your control. Explicit sharing or opening gives another app a temporary read grant; that app controls what it does with the received image. FixMyShot does not post to an account automatically.

Private export journals record frozen settings, staged references and fingerprints, owned output URIs, digests and transaction state. They permit explicit restoration/retry and avoid silently duplicating uncertain outputs after a crash. Opening or rechecking an output is read only. Accepting a verified uncertain output requires your separate confirmation; hidden, changed, missing or inaccessible output cannot be accepted through that action.

Finishing result review removes its frozen settings/source-reference journal and releases private sources no longer needed by the current draft or another pending job. Removing images or discarding a session releases its unused staged copies; it never deletes originals or saved output copies. Unresolved/corrupt recovery state can delay cleanup to protect recoverable work. The current preview retains bounded output receipts and review acknowledgements until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled; complete receipt retirement is still pending.

At the next app opening, abandoned staged files and temporary candidates older than 24 hours are eligible for cleanup. Current draft and unreviewed-job sources are protected. Android may evict cached copies earlier, which can make a draft unavailable. Cleanup is not a background promise of deletion exactly after 24 hours and is not secure erasure on flash storage. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes ordinary private state according to Android behavior; saved output copies remain.

The synthetic development test harness runs only when explicitly launched. It creates and removes its own private/provider fixtures; CI retains synthetic reports as development evidence. The installed launcher has no report-upload function. Do not include private image content, full source URIs or metadata in public bug reports. The planned privacy website has not been verified as deployed.
